# Energy & Utilities Sales Intelligence Platform

## Overview

The Energy & Utilities Sales Intelligence Platform is a lightweight business intelligence and sales opportunity management system developed using FastAPI, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

The platform helps organizations manage sales leads, monitor business opportunities, track revenue analytics, generate customer engagement content, and visualize KPIs through dashboards inspired by Power BI concepts.

This project was developed as an enterprise-style solution for Energy and Utilities business workflows.

---

## Sales Pipeline Management
- Add and manage business leads
- Track customer opportunities

- Revenue analytics
